Quick-Commerce Customer Voice

I wanted to find out why people stay with or leave India's two biggest 10-minute grocery apps. So I went through 22,651 public app reviews, tagged what each unhappy customer was complaining about, and turned it into a simple plan to win them back.

Why I did this

I use quick-commerce apps myself. I like Blinkit because it usually has what I'm looking for, while Zepto often doesn't have the items I want. I used to like Instamart for its offers, but now there are fewer offers and the same product often costs more than on Blinkit.

So I wanted to check: is my experience what most customers feel, and what actually makes people leave an app?

What surprised me

  • Stock wasn't the big issue. I expected Zepto's main problem to be missing stock, but only about 3 in 100 Zepto complaints were about it. Quality, support and missing items in the order mattered far more.
  • Fees are Blinkit's problem, not Zepto's.
  • The longer the review, the angrier it is.
  • The day of the week makes no difference to ratings.

The answer in one line

Three problems cause two in three of Zepto's "I'm leaving" reviews. Fix quality, support and missing items in the operation, then reach every unhappy customer with the right message within hours, not weeks.

My win-back plan

Each idea turns one finding into: who to target, when to message them, what to send, what to test, and how to measure it

How these campaigns would run

  • Groups: pick customers by what they did, e.g. complained, gave a low rating, or stopped ordering.
  • Message journey: push first, then WhatsApp if it isn't opened in 24 hours, without over-messaging.
  • A/B test: send two versions to two similar groups, and keep one group with no message to compare.
  • Measure: check how many come back and order again within 7 and 14 days.

Who to reach first

  • Named a competitor: Zepto users who say they're moving to Blinkit or Instamart.
  • Loyal customers at risk: long-time or pass holders who now complain (about 3 in 100 unhappy Zepto customers).
  • Long, angry reviews: 50+ word reviews average 1.25★. These are the most frustrated users.
  • Upvoted complaints: support failures that others publicly agree with.

How the analysis was built

STEP 1Collect

22,661 public Play Store & App Store reviews, taken from open datasets shared on GitHub.

STEP 2Clean

Kept reviews with at least 6 words, removed duplicates and all usernames: 22,651 left.

STEP 3Compare fairly

Compared both apps over the same days (7–17 Jul 2026): 1,057 Blinkit vs 1,147 Zepto reviews.

STEP 4Tag the complaints

Used an AI tool to tag the main complaint in all 1,437 unhappy July reviews, and mark anyone saying they'd leave.

STEP 5Check it

Ran a second, separate AI tagging round on 120 reviews: about 97 in 100 tags matched. Results also match a bigger public study.

Limitations

  • Blinkit covers only 7–17 Jul 2026, so head-to-head numbers use that window. Zepto trends use Mar–Sep 2026.
  • Longer reviews tend to be angrier than short ones, so my scores are lower than the bigger study. The gap between the two apps is what matters.
  • Reviews come from third-party public collections, English only. Blinkit's App Store IDs are generated.
  • Month-by-month trends use a simple word search. The main complaint shares use the AI tags.
